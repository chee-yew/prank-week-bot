# Prank Week Bot

A Telegram bot that lets every participant chat **anonymously** with their Pranker and their Prankee.

## How it works in 10 seconds
- You know your **Prankee**, and you prank them in secret.
- Your **Pranker** is plotting against you, and you don't know who they are.
- The bot chat has two tabs: **😈 Pranker** (secret, pranking you) and **🎯 Prankee** (named on the tab, you prank them). Open one and send anything. The bot passes it on without revealing who sent it.
